# AggiePulse curated seeds

All research retrieved **2026-11-07**. UTF-8 CSV; dates ISO; times are **America/Chicago**, including road football times as displayed by TAMU. Boolean flags are 0/1. Blank numeric/time fields mean unknown, never zero. These are hand-curated snapshots and schedules can change.

## Flag semantics

`term` is Fall 2026 from first class through commencement (August 24–December 12), otherwise Break. `is_break` marks outside that interval plus Labor Day and Thanksgiving holidays; ordinary weekends and reading days are separate, not breaks. August 1–23 can overlap summer activities: Break means outside **this fall model**, not a claim the university has no summer classes. `is_class_day` is an instructional weekday, excluding holidays/reading days/exams. `week_of_term` counts elapsed seven-day weeks beginning August 24 and remains populated through commencement, including weekends/holidays. `days_to_finals` is signed calendar days to December 7 for every row; negative after that date. Commencement does not imply classes.
